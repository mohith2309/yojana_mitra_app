## 2024-07-25 - [Flutter IconButton Accessibility]
**Learning:** In Flutter, `IconButton` widgets require a `tooltip` property to be accessible to screen readers (equivalent to `aria-label`). Without it, icon-only buttons are completely opaque to assistive technologies.
**Action:** Always ensure any `IconButton` or icon-only interactive widget includes a descriptive `tooltip` property.
